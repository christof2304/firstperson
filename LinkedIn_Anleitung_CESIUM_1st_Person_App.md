# CESIUM 1st Person App — Quick Guide

🕶️ **CESIUM 1st Person App** – experience Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles up close, in real time, right in your browser.

📍 Location in this example: Messe München (Riem)

**How to use it:**
1. Load the script into Cesium Sandcastle and run it
2. Click **"Activate First-Person"** (or press **C**) – you're instantly inside the 3D scene
3. Move with **WASD**, go up/down with **Q/E**, look around with the mouse
4. **Shift** = speed boost
5. Gamepad connected? Auto-detected – left stick moves, right stick looks, **A** toggles first-person, triggers for up/down

**More shortcuts:**
- **X** – toggle shadows
- **Q** – cycle quality (Ultra → High → Medium → Low)
- **R** – reset to auto quality
- **P** – log current camera pose to console

⚙️ Runs smoothly even on leaner hardware (optimized for 6 GB VRAM) and automatically adapts quality & resolution to maintain frame rate.
